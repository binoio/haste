Haste

A personal finance suite for the sole purpose of recovery and viewing data from popular Deluxe finance software circa 2000.

Download for macOS
Version 1.0.3 Apple Silicon Native (arm64) macOS Monterey 12.0+ Free & Open Source

Historical Dataset & QDF Inspector

Real-time binary inspection of classic .QDF, .QSD, and .QEL datasets, automatic companion QIF detection, and skeleton account import.

Authentic Checkbook Register

Classic dual-line and green-bar ledger styling, inline transaction entry, instant running balance calculation, cleared status toggling, and split transaction support.

100% Offline & Private

Your financial data stays entirely on your Mac in ~/Library/Application Support/Haste. Zero cloud trackers, zero analytics, and zero subscription fees.
